Ultimate Lan Gaem

4v4 tower defense autobattler

HomeSupportPrivacy

Ultimate Lan Gaem is a 4v4 tower defense autobattler made for playing together. Every Player defends their own Lane against shared Waves of Enemies, upgrades and Evolves their Towers, and sends units into the Opponent's Lane. The last Team with a standing Base wins.

Features

  • 40 Towers, each with its own Upgrade Paths and Evolutions
  • Six Elements to pick: Fire, Ice, Nature, Arcane, Shadow and Tech
  • Up to 8 players on the same Wi-Fi, or over the internet by IP
  • Bots for empty seats or solo play, and a short Tutorial
  • Phones and PCs in the same match
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no account
