Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Ultimate Lan Gaem does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • During a match the game exchanges game data (your display name and your moves) directly with the other players' devices in that match. It is not sent to us or to any server we run.
  • Local Network access is used only to find and join matches on your network.
  • Settings and tutorial progress are saved only on your device.

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, what you write is public on GitHub and handled under GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions: Support.
